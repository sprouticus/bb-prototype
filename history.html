Our History

It began with a cousin, a hunting trip, and a green mail Jeep that could be boarded straight from the curb. Four decades later, that same idea gets welded together by hand in Albuquerque, and in 2027 it goes out to its first customers.

Scroll Down

An Idea is Sparked (1987)

Our founder and his cousin Ray hunted together for years. Ray had been in a car accident that left him with lasting injuries, and getting him to the field was always the hard part. So our founder rebuilt a retired mail Jeep, green and unglamorous, to be boarded straight from the curb. It was nothing fancy. But it worked.

The Buddy Buggy is Born (2017)

Thirty years is a long time to hold onto an idea. When it finally got built we called it the Buddy Buggy, for Ray. Rear wheelchair lift, electric drive, room to roll straight in from the chair. The lift was the part that mattered: getting in took nobody's help but the driver's. It earned its keep on hunting trips, farms and ranches.

From Buddy Buggy to Enabler (2023)

COVID closed the shop and the frame sat waiting. Nobody else built one in the meantime, so we went back to it. All-wheel drive, all-steel, a self-loading rear lift the driver works alone. The lift also comes off in four bolts, so the vehicle is never tied to one person: anyone in the family or on the crew can drive it. By then it was doing real work, and it got a name to match.

The Enabler Evolves (2025)

Every change on the 2.1 came from watching the 2.0 work. Bigger mirrors and more cameras, because a driver who can't turn to look behind shouldn't have to guess what's back there, least of all while towing. Bigger wheels for a gentler ride. A roof that stays taut instead of flapping. And jump seats: the 2.0 carried its driver, the 2.1 carries three.

Ready to Roll (2027)

Forty years after a green Jeep in a driveway, the Enabler 2.1 goes out to its first customers. Every one is welded, wired and finished by hand in Albuquerque. We're proud of each one that leaves the shop, and we can't wait to see where they end up.
