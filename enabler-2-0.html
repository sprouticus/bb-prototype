The Enabler

A work vehicle built to adapt

A heavy-duty, all-electric utility vehicle with a self-loading lift and a catch-and-release hitch. Designed to adapt to any driver and any job.

Now Reserving

The Enabler 2.1

The Enabler 2.1, in production now.

The 2.1 uses the same chassis and drivetrain with more capacity, a bigger motor, better rear visibility and a roof option. It starts shipping to customers in 2027. Pre-ordering takes a few minutes, and the first 50 pre-orders get the base vehicle at $34,000.

New and Improved

Key updates, expanded passenger capacity, and performance upgrades in the Enabler 2.1 build.

  • CapacityJump seats in back bring capacity to three.
  • MotorA bigger motor with more torque, up to a top speed of 25 miles an hour.
  • VisibilityA better rear camera with a larger viewing screen on the dashboard, and optional West Coast mirrors for towing.
  • RoofA curved canvas roof on tensioning rods to keep the elements out.

NO PAYMENT REQUIRED

The Enabler is built for off-road use only. It is not rated or sold for operation on public roads. Assembled in Albuquerque, New Mexico, from domestic and imported components.

Built-In Access

The lift, self-operated.

The lift is what makes solo operation possible. It lowers to ground level, the driver boards on their own, and it raises them into the seat. No spotter, no transfer, no one else needed to get the vehicle moving.

Runs More Than the Cab

Mount implements on the front end.

Front-mounted implements are an available add-on. The front winch and connecting plate mount heavy tools directly to the vehicle, including brush cutters, flail mowers, lawn mowers, and snowblowers, all controlled from the cab.
