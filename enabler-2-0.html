Our Journey

From the Buddy Buggy to the Enabler

The vehicle has evolved, but the principle remains unchanged: a heavy-duty utility vehicle that adapts to any driver, giving wheelchair users direct, unassisted access to the jobsite, trail, and field.

Now Reserving

The Enabler 2.1

The Enabler 2.1, in production now.

The 2.1 uses the same chassis and drivetrain with more capacity, a bigger motor, better rear visibility and a roof option. Pre-ordering takes a few minutes and costs nothing.

What's New

Key updates, expanded passenger capacity, and performance upgrades in the Enabler 2.1 build.

  • CapacityJump seats in back bring capacity to three.
  • MotorA bigger motor with more torque, up to a top speed of 25 miles an hour.
  • VisibilityA better rear camera with a larger viewing screen on the dashboard, and larger West Coast mirrors for towing.
  • RoofA curved canvas roof on tensioning rods, so it stays taut. The current model has none.

Built-In Access

The lift, self-operated.

The lift is what makes solo operation possible. It lowers to ground level, the driver boards on their own, and it raises them into the seat. No spotter, no transfer, no one else needed to get the vehicle moving.

Why the Enabler Is a Workhorse

Catch-and-Release: a quick-attach hitch for towed implements.

Catch-and-Release is an available add-on. Adding it to the vehicle is what makes the towed implements work. Mowers, sprayers, blades and trailers hook up from the driver's seat. No transfer, no help needed.
