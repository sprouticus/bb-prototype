Why the Enabler Is a Workhorse

Catch-and-Release: a quick-attach hitch for towed implements.

Catch-and-Release is our quick-connect implement hitch. A spring-loaded hook catches a pin on the implement tongue, and a lever in the cab lets it go. Mowers, sprayers and trailers hook up from the driver's seat.

Why Now

1.5M more disabled workers have joined the U.S. workforce since 2020.

Employment among disabled Americans is at a record 22 percent. Only 3 to 4 percent of disabled workers get any equipment-based accommodation. Most outdoor work has no equipment built for a wheelchair user at all.
