Add-Ons & Upgrades

Accessories

Bolt-on upgrades for seating, visibility, lighting, cargo, and recovery.

Multifunctional by Design

Adaptable to any driver, now and in the future.

Seating, visibility, lighting, and cargo are all specified when the vehicle is built, and every one of them can be changed later. An Enabler set up for one driver can be reconfigured for another as an organization's crew changes, so it keeps working for whoever is driving it.

Built-In Cargo

Diamond-plate cargo bed with integrated tie-downs, standard on every build.

Lighting & Suspension

Off-road LED lighting and coil-over suspension, specified per driver and per job.

Finish Details

Every light, handle, and fastener is chosen to match how the vehicle will be used.
