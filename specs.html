05 Pricing

$34,000 is the base vehicle for the first 50 pre-orders. Accessory and implement pricing is being finalized with each supplier. Nothing is collected at pre-order. 50% comes due when an order is confirmed and a build is scheduled.

The Enabler is built for off-road use only. It is not rated or sold for operation on public roads. Maximum speed 25 mph. Whether any low-speed vehicle may be operated on a public road is decided by state and local law, not by us.

Designed in the U.S.A. and assembled in Albuquerque, New Mexico, from domestic and imported components. We source domestically wherever we can.

In the Shop

Engineered and welded in-house.

Every Enabler is designed, welded and assembled by our own team in Albuquerque. The chassis is built inhouse, with no contract assembly. Floor panels and major components unbolt, so a unit can be fixed on site.
