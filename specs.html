Technical Data Sheet

Enabler Full Specs

Full specs for the Enabler. Every unit shares the same chassis and drivetrain.

01 Power

SystemAll-electric, 51.2V
BatteryLithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4), deep-cycle
Battery capacity150 Ah
Accessory battery1 × 12V
Motor torque110 ft-lb

02 Suspension & Driveline

Front suspensionIndependent, disc brakes
Rear axleStandard, with shocks and disc brakes
Gearbox2-speed, high/low with reverse
SteeringPower-assist
Wheels5-lug mounting
Tires14" or 16"
Maximum speed25 mph
Use classificationOff-road industrial & utility use. Engineered for private land, jobsites, and municipal grounds

03 Weight & Dimensions

Weight2,000 lb
Length124"
Width67"
Height77.5"
Ground clearance8.25"
Wheelbase98"

04 Features & Equipment

  • All-wheel drive
  • Rear, self-operated wheelchair lift
  • Multifunctional and adaptable to multiple drivers. Pull the rear lift, four bolts and two wires, and any driver can use it
  • Optional docking for manual or battery-powered wheelchairs
  • All-steel construction for rugged, extended use
  • Designed in the U.S.A. and assembled in Albuquerque, New Mexico
  • Optional dual folding rear seats for passengers
  • Standard mirrors, brake lights, turn signals, headlights
  • Optional standard driver's seat (swap from wheelchair-based operation)
  • 2,000 lb front winch
  • Mountable high-lift jack
  • Rear work lights
  • Split front windshield
  • Charging port for accessories & personal electronics
  • Heavy-duty rubber traction mat for wheelchair-based operators

Want the full lineup of add-ons and tow-behind tools? See Accessories and Implements.

05 Pricing

Base vehicle, first 50 pre-orders$34,000
Deposit at pre-orderNone
Due at order confirmation50%

$34,000 is the base vehicle for the first 50 pre-orders. Accessory and implement pricing is being finalized with each supplier. Nothing is collected at pre-order. 50% comes due when an order is confirmed and a build is scheduled.

The Enabler is built for off-road use only. It is not rated or sold for operation on public roads. Maximum speed 25 mph. Whether any low-speed vehicle may be operated on a public road is decided by state and local law, not by us.

Designed in the U.S.A. and assembled in Albuquerque, New Mexico, from domestic and imported components. We source domestically wherever we can.

In the Shop

Engineered and welded in-house.

Every Enabler is designed, welded and assembled by our own team in Albuquerque. The chassis is built inhouse, with no contract assembly. Floor panels and major components unbolt, so a unit can be fixed on site.
