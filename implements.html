Towing & Front-Mounted Tools

Implements

The Enabler is a workhorse designed to support towing implements from Demco and Kunz and front-mounted implements from Rammy.

Built to Connect

Implements mount front and rear.

Towed implements run off the Catch-and-Release hitch. It couples on contact, so going from a mower to a sprayer to a trailer happens from the driver's seat, with no spotter and no hardware to swap between brands. Front-mounted implements run off the winch. Both are add-ons to the vehicle.

Towing Implements

With the catch-and-release added on to the vehicle, the Enabler can tow trailers, sprayers, mowers, or custom implements of your choice. All implement purchases from BB Engineering include a remote-controlled electric jack to keep the pin aligned and the driver in the seat.
