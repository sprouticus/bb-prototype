Built to Connect

Towed implements all run off the same hitch.

Catch-and-Release couples on contact. Going from a mower to a sprayer to a trailer happens from the driver's seat, with no spotter and no hardware to swap between brands. Front-mounted implements connect differently — see below.

Towing Implements

Catch-and-Release, mounted on the back of the Enabler, guides the pin on the implement tongue into position from side to side. A remote-controlled electric jack on the tongue raises and lowers it to meet the catch. The jack is included with every implement purchase, and the whole hookup happens without leaving the seat.

Front-Mounted Implements

Front-mounted implements do not use Catch-and-Release. They attach to a solid base plate that extends from under the Enabler, and the front winch raises and lowers them from a button in the cab. The winch is available as an add-on.
