Watch It Work

Catch-and-Release

A quick-attach hitch for towed implements, worked from the driver's seat.

The driver never has to get out to hook up.

How It Works

Three steps, all from the seat.

A spring-loaded hook on the vehicle, a pin on the implement tongue, one lever in the cab.

Step 01

Line Up

Back toward the tongue. The catch takes the pin from a range of angles, no need to hit it dead center. A backup camera sits in the catch bracket.

Step 02

Catch

A fob raises or lowers the tongue on its electric jack until the pin sits at the right height. Back in, and the pin slides into the catch from the side, where a spring-loaded hook takes it. That's the whole hookup.

Step 03

Release

The lever lifts the hook off the pin. Pull forward and the implement stays where it was set down.
