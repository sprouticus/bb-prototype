The Vehicle

Catch-and-Release

Hooking up a trailer is a standing job. You lift the tongue, line it up over the ball, and drop the pin. Catch-and-Release is the hitch we built so none of that has to happen, and so the driver never has to get out to start working.

Watch It Work

Back in, catch the pin, pull the lever.

Filmed on the lot where every Enabler is built and tested. The trailer goes on, the vehicle drives off with it, and the trailer comes back off. Nobody gets out, and nobody else is standing there to help.

How It Works

Three moves, all of them from the driver's seat.

The mechanism is deliberately simple. A spring-loaded hook on the vehicle, a pin on the implement tongue, and one lever inside the cab.

Step 01

Line Up

Back the vehicle toward the tongue. The catch takes the pin from a range of angles, so you don't have to hit it dead center on the first try.

Step 02

Catch

The hook drops over the pin as the vehicle makes contact and holds it there. That is the entire hookup. Nothing to lift, nothing to thread, nothing to bolt.

Step 03

Release

When the work is done, the lever in the cab lifts the hook off the pin. Pull forward and the implement stays exactly where you set it down.
